Quarterly Activities Report — Q3 2026
St. Joseph Parish, Ouagadougou

1. Catechist Training
Three training sessions were held in September 2026 for 45 catechists across the parish. Topics covered: lesson planning, child safeguarding, and the use of digital tools. Attendance increased by 12% compared to the second quarter.

2. Youth Program
The youth choir and study groups met weekly. A retreat was organized on 14 September with 60 participants. The main challenge remains the lack of transport for attendees living in rural areas.

3. Finances
Total income for the quarter was 1,850,000 FCFA, mainly from Sunday collections and donations. Expenses reached 1,620,000 FCFA, leaving a surplus of 230,000 FCFA allocated to the renovation fund.

4. Risks and Outlook
The main risk for the next quarter is the rainy season, which may reduce attendance at outdoor activities. Mitigation: schedule outdoor events before noon and confirm transport arrangements in advance.
